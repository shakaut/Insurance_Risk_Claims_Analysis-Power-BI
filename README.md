# Car Insurance Risk & Claims Analysis

## Project Overview
Developed an interactive insurance analytics dashboard to analyze claim patterns, customer risk factors, and policy trends across demographics, vehicle characteristics, and geographic coverage zones. The dashboard helps identify high-risk segments, optimize premiums, and support data-driven insurance decisions.

# Key Business Insights

### 1. Private Vehicles Generate the Highest Claims
- Private-use vehicles contributed approximately **$150.4M** in claims compared to **$37.4M** from commercial vehicles.
- Indicates higher exposure in personal auto insurance portfolios.

### 2. Ford and Chevrolet Have the Highest Claim Amounts
- Top claim-generating manufacturers:
  - Ford: **$16.6M**
  - Chevrolet: **$14.8M**
- Suggests higher repair costs or increased accident frequency.

### 3. Urban Areas Show Higher Claim Exposure
- Urban and highly urban zones recorded the highest total claim amounts (~**$38M** each).
- Dense traffic conditions likely increase accident probability.

### 4. Claim Amounts Increased After 2000
- Claims remained stable during the 1990s but increased significantly after 2000.
- Peak claim amounts occurred between **2012–2018**.

### 5. Customers with No Kids Driving Produce Maximum Claims
- Policyholders without children drivers generated the highest claim volume (~**$134M**).
- Claim amounts decreased as the number of kids driving increased.

### 6. Bachelor Degree Holders Account for Highest Claims
- Education-based claim distribution:
  - Bachelors: **$94M**
  - High School: **$51.8M**
  - Masters: **$28.3M**
  - PhD: **$13.7M**

### 7. Married Customers Generate High Claim Amounts
- Married individuals contributed approximately **$50.6M** in claims.
- Marital status can be useful for risk segmentation and underwriting analysis.
